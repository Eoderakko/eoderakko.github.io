Eoderakko

Tell your taxi driver where to go in Korea.

Beta testing

The problem

When foreign visitors take a taxi in Korea, they often say a restaurant or shop name in their own language. Drivers frequently cannot understand it, and the ride starts with confusion.

How it works

1Choose your language.
2Say or type the place name.
3Show the driver the Korean name on screen.

AI identifies the place you mean and displays its Korean name in large text, so the driver can read it right away.

Supported languages

日本語 中文 English

Status

Eoderakko is currently in beta testing with a small number of taxi drivers in Korea.
